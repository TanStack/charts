# AG Charts pricing and licensing notes

**Retrieved**: 2026-07-26  
**Sources**:

- https://www.ag-grid.com/charts/license-pricing/

## Packages

- AG Charts Community: free, MIT licensed, production use allowed.
- AG Charts Enterprise: commercial licence.
- Enterprise Bundle: AG Grid Enterprise plus AG Charts Enterprise.

## Public list prices

| Offer             |                 Price | Terms shown                                |
| ----------------- | --------------------: | ------------------------------------------ |
| Community         |                  Free | MIT                                        |
| Charts Enterprise |   From $499/developer | Perpetual; one year of support and updates |
| Enterprise Bundle | From $1,498/developer | Perpetual; one year of support and updates |

The site offers a 30-day Enterprise Bundle trial without restrictions or
watermarks. Unlicensed Enterprise evaluation otherwise displays console
warnings and a temporary watermark.

## Monetisation boundary

Community includes the common series and baseline chart affordances.
Enterprise is monetised around:

- Financial, map, gauge, hierarchy, flow, range, and statistical series
- Zoom, navigator, crosshairs, context menus, annotations, synchronisation, and
  other end-user analysis controls
- Server-side raster rendering
- Dedicated Zendesk support and priority fixes

## Interpretation

The free product is sufficient to compete for ordinary dashboard charts. The
paid boundary is placed around specialised visualisations, advanced
interaction, and enterprise support rather than basic rendering.
