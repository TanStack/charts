## Directional themes

1. Performance is moving from initial rendering into continuous, interactive,
   million-point and remote-data workflows.
2. End-user analytical state is becoming first-class: zoom, navigator,
   selection, annotations, scrollbars, range buttons, and synchronisation.
3. The product is expanding beyond classic charts into finance, maps,
   hierarchy, flows, gauges, and organisation charts.
4. Developer experience is receiving investment through modules, TypeScript
   generics, simpler axes, CSS variables, state APIs, migration guides, and LTS.
5. The release train is active: a major or minor approximately every six to
   eight weeks in this sample.
