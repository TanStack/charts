Add a Solid 2 adapter at `@tanstack/charts/solid/solid2` (and `@tanstack/solid-charts/solid2`). It takes the same props and exports the same types as the Solid 1 adapter, which is unchanged at `@tanstack/charts/solid`. The `solid-js` peer now accepts `^1.8 || >=2.0.0-rc.19 <3.0.0`, with an optional `@solidjs/web` peer for the Solid 2 entry.
